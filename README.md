### Emilio Navarro García

Psychology graduate and developer based in Madrid. I have managed my own investment portfolio since 2016, and most of what I build here comes from that: tools to test market ideas against historical data.

- **[Termómetro](https://github.com/seakerop/termometro)**: tests 26 market indicators against future S&P 500 returns (data since 1881) and 28 against Bitcoin, using block bootstrap and Benjamini–Hochberg correction. [Report](https://seakerop.github.io/termometro/)
- **[Vigía](https://github.com/seakerop/vigia)**: market alerts with a tester that checks what happened the previous times each signal fired.
- **[Bóveda](https://github.com/seakerop/boveda)**: personal net worth tracker with encrypted local storage.
